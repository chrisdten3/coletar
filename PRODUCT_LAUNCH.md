## Phase 0: Friends & debugging

Before any pitching, get 10 of our friends to use Coleta and debug problems fully for free. Use this phase to find and fix real issues before we put the product in front of anyone we're trying to convert.

From there, we move on to pitching businesses, minor development orgs, etc.
